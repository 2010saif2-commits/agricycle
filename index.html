Every field is different.

The soil, the weather, the water, and even the crops grown in previous seasons all play a part in what comes next. Yet choosing the right crop isn't always straightforward.

Farmers often have to make decisions based on experience, changing conditions, and information that isn't always easy to find.

That's where AgriCycle comes in.

AgriCycle is a smart crop-planning platform designed to help farmers make more informed decisions about what to grow, when to grow it, and how to look after their land.

By bringing together information about soil conditions, local climate, previous crops, and environmental data, AgriCycle aims to turn scattered information into practical guidance that farmers can actually use.

We believe better farming doesn't necessarily mean doing more. Sometimes, it simply means making better decisions with what you already have.

Our Purpose

To make agricultural information easier to understand and more useful in everyday farming.

We want to help farmers look beyond a single growing season and think about the long-term health of their land. From choosing suitable crops to understanding crop rotation, our goal is to make planning more thoughtful and accessible.

What We Focus On

01

Understanding the Land

Every good farming decision starts with knowing the field. Soil type, fertility, moisture, and local conditions all matter.

02

Choosing the Right Crop

Not every crop belongs in every field. We aim to help farmers identify crops that are better suited to their land and growing conditions.

03

Planning Beyond One Season

What grows today can affect what grows tomorrow. Crop rotation helps farmers plan more balanced growing seasons and manage soil nutrients.

04

Making Information Useful

Climate and environmental data can be difficult to interpret. AgriCycle brings relevant information together to make it easier to understand and apply.

Our Vision

We imagine a future where farmers have access to the information they need to make confident decisions, where technology supports agricultural experience rather than replaces it, and where the health of the land remains just as important as the harvest.

Because a successful harvest is important. But keeping the land ready for the next one matters just as much.

Our Services

Bringing together data, climate insights, and soil science to help you make better decisions for your land.

01

Smart Crop Recommendations

Find crops that make sense for your land.

Choosing a crop shouldn't be a guessing game. AgriCycle uses information such as your location, soil characteristics, temperature, moisture and growing conditions to help identify crops that may be suitable for your field.

Our aim is simple: help you make a more informed choice before the growing season begins.

02

Crop Rotation Planning

Think about the next harvest, not just the current one.

The crop you grow today can influence the condition of your soil tomorrow. AgriCycle takes previous crop history into account to help farmers plan suitable crop sequences and make better use of their land over time.

Because good farming is about planning ahead.

03

Soil Condition Analysis

Understand what your soil can support.

Healthy crops begin with suitable soil conditions. Soil type, moisture and nutrient availability all influence how well a crop can grow.

AgriCycle brings available soil information together to help farmers understand their field's characteristics and consider crops that match those conditions.

04

Climate and Environmental Insights

Know the conditions surrounding your farm.

Weather and environmental conditions play a major role in agriculture. Through available environmental datasets, including NASA's Earth observation and meteorological resources, AgriCycle aims to provide useful information about temperature, rainfall, humidity and other relevant conditions.

This information helps put crop decisions into a wider environmental context.

05

Seasonal Crop Planning

Make your next growing season count.

Timing matters just as much as crop selection. AgriCycle helps farmers consider seasonal conditions and crop requirements when planning what to cultivate and when.

The goal is to make each growing season part of a more thoughtful, long-term plan.

06

Land and Location-Based Assessment

Because no two fields are exactly alike.

A recommendation that works in one district may not work in another. AgriCycle uses farm location and available regional agricultural information to make its assessments more relevant to the conditions of a particular area.

From the wider region down to the individual farm, location helps provide context for better decisions.

Satellite Mission

Soil Moisture from Space

NASA's Soil Moisture Active Passive (SMAP) mission provides soil-moisture data collected through satellite observations.

AgriCycle uses this information to assess water availability in agricultural land and support more informed crop recommendations.
